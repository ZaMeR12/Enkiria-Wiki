## Team Planner (fully custom)

This tool was made quickly with some help of AI to follow the demands to have a Team planner that be able to accept custom pokemon typing, stats, abillities and moves. The only things that it's can't manage it's pokemon image for the team.

!!! note
    This tool save your team by url queries. So it's easy to share your team with other trainers.
