## Entry
This Shuckle variant has adapted to the toxic flora of Enkiria's shadowy valleys. Instead of fermenting berries into juice, it distills potent plant-based compounds within its shell — some act as powerful sedatives, others as deadly poisons. Alchemists and rogue healers alike seek it out, though handling it requires extreme caution. Its shell is often warm to the touch, hinting at the volatile chemical reactions brewing inside.
