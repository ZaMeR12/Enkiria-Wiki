## Pixelmon

??? question "Why are some Enkirian forms not available in the wild?"
    Some Enkirian forms are only distributed during special events. 


??? question "Are Enkirian forms made for competitive?"
    We tried to make them viable in competitive battles without making them too OP.
    We hope you will use them in your competitive team happily.

??? question "Why are some sprites of Enkirian forms teal or red?"
    We don't always have the budget to pay artist to recolor the sprites correctly. So we made the choice to colorize them to teal (normal palette) and red (shiny palette) to be easier to see if you have an Enkirian form in your team or PC, as placeholders.
